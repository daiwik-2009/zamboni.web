Skating Unit 2: Edge Work Control Est. Time: 15 mins

Mastering the Inside Edge Cut

Powerful skating relies heavily on maximizing your blade contact. For a sharp inside edge turn, lower your center of gravity by flexing your knees over your toes. Push through the heel of your inside foot while anchoring the weight on your core to initiate a seamless pivot direction swap without shedding momentum.

Stickhandling Unit 4: Puck Mechanics Est. Time: 12 mins

The Top Hand Expansion Sweep

Your top hand on the stick controls the angle and rotational dexterity of your blade, while the bottom hand acts primarily as a loose guide support stabilizer. Rolling your top wrist smoothly allows you to cushion the puck softly on both the forehand and backhand sides without making audible tapping sounds against the ice surface.

Shooting Unit 1: Wrist Shot Velocity Est. Time: 18 mins

Flex Loading and Downward Force

Modern composite sticks are designed to act like a dynamic spring catapult system. To generate high velocity on a wrist shot, don't just sweep the puck forward horizontally. Press your weight downward directly into the ice shaft slightly *before* making contact with the puck to load flex potential energy into the stick frame.
